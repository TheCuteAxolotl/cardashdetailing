# Car Dash V5.1 — Booking Add-Ons, Contact Services, Headlight Pricing, Discounts

## Booking add-ons now affect the exact total
The booking form shows each active Car Detailing add-on with its price. Selecting an add-on immediately updates the price breakdown and final booking total.

Default add-on pricing includes:
- Pet Hair Removal — $39
- Seat Shampoo — $49
- Carpet Extraction — $59
- Heavy Stain Removal — $69
- Leather Protection — $39
- Odor Treatment — $49
- Engine Bay Cleaning — $49
- Iron Decontamination — $45
- Clay Bar Treatment — $59
- Hand Wax — $49
- Ceramic Sealant — $79
- Wheel Coating — $89
- Exterior Trim Protection — $45
- Bug & Tar Removal — $35
- Underbody Cleaning — $30
- Headlight Restoration with a detail — $80

The server recalculates add-ons before saving a booking, so editing the price in the browser cannot change the saved booking total.

## Headlight Restoration
- Booked by itself: $99 by default.
- Added to a compatible car detail: $80 by default.
- Both prices can be managed from Owner → Add-Ons & Discounts.

## Contact / booking service dropdown
The booking form now lists:
- Car Detailing Packages
- Exterior Detailing packages
- Interior Detailing packages
- Headlight Restoration
- All active services from Owner → Services

Packages let the customer choose Coupe, Sedan, or Truck & SUV directly in the booking form and show the exact current price.
Services that still require an estimate/quote appear in the dropdown but direct the customer to Chat to a Specialist instead of allowing an unpriced booking.

## Discount codes
Owner Dashboard now includes **Add-Ons & Discounts** at `/owner/booking-settings`.
The owner can:
- Create percentage discount codes
- Create fixed-dollar discount codes
- Activate/deactivate codes
- Delete codes
- Add, remove, enable, disable, rename, and reprice booking add-ons
- Change the standalone Headlight Restoration price

Discount codes are validated server-side. The private discount-code list is not included in the public `/api/site-content` response.

## Database
No Prisma schema migration is required. Add-on configuration and discount-code configuration use the existing `SiteContent` table.

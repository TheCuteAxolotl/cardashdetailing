# V5.1.6 — Two-Way SMS + Arrival Updates

## What changed

- Added inbound Twilio SMS webhook at `/api/sms/inbound`.
- Customer replies are matched to their newest active booking by normalized phone number.
- Incoming SMS replies are saved into the existing booking conversation.
- Added `SMS Inbox` pages for Owner and Admin dashboards.
- Unread customer replies show a badge in the dashboard and inbox.
- Opening a booking conversation marks customer replies as seen by staff.
- Staff replies from booking chat are sent directly as SMS when the booking has SMS consent.
- Added `On my way SMS` and `Send arrival time` actions to Owner > Bookings.
- Booking chat now labels messages that were actually sent/received through SMS.
- Added Twilio webhook signature validation and MessageSid deduplication.

## Required Twilio setup

After this build is deployed, configure Twilio to POST inbound messages to:

`https://cardashdetailing.com/api/sms/inbound`

If your public production URL is different, use that exact URL instead.

### Option A — Messaging Service

In Twilio Console:

1. Messaging > Services
2. Open the Messaging Service used by the website.
3. Open Integration / Incoming Message Handling.
4. Configure incoming messages to use a webhook.
5. Method: `POST`
6. URL: `https://cardashdetailing.com/api/sms/inbound`

### Option B — Phone-number webhook

If the Messaging Service is set to defer to the sender's webhook:

1. Phone Numbers > Manage > Active Numbers
2. Open the Twilio number used for Car Dash Detailing.
3. Under Messaging, set `A message comes in` to Webhook.
4. Method: `POST`
5. URL: `https://cardashdetailing.com/api/sms/inbound`

## Environment variables

The existing variables are still required:

- `TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID`
- `TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN`
- `TWILIO_MESSAGING_SERVICE_SID`
- `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://cardashdetailing.com`

Optional:

- `TWILIO_INBOUND_WEBHOOK_URL=https://cardashdetailing.com/api/sms/inbound`

`TWILIO_INBOUND_WEBHOOK_URL` is useful if the exact webhook URL Twilio calls differs from `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`.

## How it works

1. Car Dash sends a booking/status/arrival text through Twilio.
2. The customer replies to the same Twilio number.
3. Twilio POSTs the reply to `/api/sms/inbound`.
4. The website validates the Twilio signature.
5. The customer's phone number is normalized and matched to the latest confirmed/pending booking.
6. The reply is saved into that booking's conversation with channel `sms`.
7. Owner/Admin sees it in `SMS Inbox` and can open the conversation.
8. A staff reply from booking chat is sent back through Twilio as a real SMS and also saved in the conversation.

## Arrival update examples

**On my way**

`Car Dash Detailing: Your detailer is on the way. We’ll see you soon!`

**Arrival time**

`Car Dash Detailing: Your detailer is on the way and is expected to arrive around 2:30 PM. We’ll see you soon!`

## Matching behavior

When a phone number has multiple bookings, inbound SMS replies are assigned in this order:

1. Most recently updated confirmed booking
2. Most recently updated pending booking
3. Most recently updated booking of any status

This is designed for a small mobile-detailing workflow where one customer phone number normally has one active appointment at a time.

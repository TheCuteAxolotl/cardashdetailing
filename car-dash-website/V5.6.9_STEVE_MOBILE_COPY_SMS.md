# v5.6.9 — Mobile convenience copy + custom quote wording

## Public copy updates
- Keeps the homepage headline: **Mobile detailing that comes to you.**
- Reworks the homepage body to emphasize convenience: **You pick the service. You pick the time.**
- Changes the homepage quote CTA from **Need an exact quote?** to **Need a custom quote?**
- Changes the quote-page headline from **Get a Free Exact Quote** to **Get a Free Custom Quote** for both guest and signed-in views.
- Rewrites quote-page intro copy around customizing the quote to the vehicle, condition, and requested work.

## Booking confirmation communication
- No confirmation email was added.
- The existing SMS flow remains in place for customers who consent to transactional texts:
  - booking-request received text after submission;
  - confirmation text when staff changes the booking to Confirmed;
  - replies can continue by SMS / booking chat.
- No automatic confirmation phone call was added.
- The booking form now makes the SMS behavior clearer: customers can opt in to receive their booking confirmation and appointment updates by text.

## Compatibility
- Existing owner-written site copy is preserved.
- The previous stock homepage hero body is mapped to the new v5.6.9 copy so sites still using the old default receive the update automatically.

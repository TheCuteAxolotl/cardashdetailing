# Car Dash V4.5

## Marine split
- `/services` is now automotive-only.
- `/marine-detailing` is a dedicated marine page with marine services, add-ons, pricing note, and 20-foot planning examples.
- Desktop, mobile, and footer links now route marine customers to the dedicated page.

## iPhone/mobile navigation
- Services and Explore are collapsible dropdown/accordion boxes on mobile.
- The mobile navigation has its own touch-scroll container for iPhone/Safari.
- Body scrolling is locked while the menu is open so swipes scroll the menu rather than the page behind it.
- The floating Need Help launcher is hidden while the mobile navigation is open so it cannot cover links.

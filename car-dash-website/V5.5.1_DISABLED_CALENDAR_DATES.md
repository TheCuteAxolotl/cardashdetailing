# v5.5.1 — Disabled booking calendar dates

- Replaced the native mobile date picker in the public booking form with a Car Dash calendar.
- Dates that are unavailable in Booking Settings are gray and cannot be tapped.
- Past dates are gray and cannot be tapped.
- Dates where every configured time is already taken are gray and cannot be tapped.
- A date remains selectable as long as at least one configured appointment time is still open.
- The calendar checks monthly availability from the server, so manual/off-site bookings also block the date once all of its times are filled.
- Public booking requests now reject past dates server-side as well.

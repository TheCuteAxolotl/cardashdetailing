# v5.4.3 — SMS Consent Clarity

Updated the Get an Exact Quote SMS opt-in language so customers immediately understand what they are agreeing to.

## Changes
- Replaced technical “transactional updates” wording with plain-language consent.
- Clearly states **no spam or promotional messages** through this opt-in.
- Explains that texts are limited to the customer’s quote, scheduling, appointment updates, or directly related service communication.
- Keeps required messaging disclosures: message frequency, message/data rates, STOP, HELP, and consent-not-required-for-purchase language.
- Applied consistently to both guest and signed-in quote request flows.
- Renamed the signed-in phone field label to “Mobile number for quote and appointment texts (optional).”

No database, API, Twilio, pricing, booking, or staff-permission logic was changed.

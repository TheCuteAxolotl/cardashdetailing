# Car Dash Detailing v5.4.0

## Public-site professionalization + SEO/GEO

This release focuses on reducing lead friction, making the public site easier for search engines and AI search systems to understand, and replacing generic marketing copy with language grounded in how Car Dash actually prices and performs detailing work.

### Guest exact quotes

- **Get an Exact Quote no longer requires an account.**
- New leads can submit name, email, phone, service, vehicle, condition, notes, and up to three photos.
- Mobile photos are compressed in the browser before upload.
- Guest submissions are rate-limited and photo payloads are validated server-side.
- The Owner/Staff Quote Inbox shows guest contact and vehicle information with Call, Text, and Email shortcuts.
- Staff can send an exact price to a guest quote by SMS when the customer opted in.
- Guest SMS replies can flow back into the guest Quote Chat instead of becoming an unmatched text.
- After submitting, the customer is offered an **optional** account that can claim the existing quote. Existing customers can sign in and claim it instead.
- Once linked to an account and accepted, the exact quote continues through the existing booking flow.

### Search visibility

- Added page-specific titles and meta descriptions to the main public pages.
- Added canonical URLs.
- Added `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` using Next.js metadata routes.
- Private/API routes are excluded from crawling.
- Added LocalBusiness structured data with Car Dash's business phone, service area, service catalog, and social profiles.
- Added Service structured data to core detailing pages.
- Added Breadcrumb structured data.
- Added FAQ structured data generated from the same FAQ content used on the site.
- Added internal related-service navigation to the main service pages.

### Public copy and local relevance

- Rewrote the main public defaults to sound like an actual mobile detailing business instead of generic template copy.
- Added clear South Elgin/local-service language without stuffing city names into every heading.
- Pricing copy now explains that vehicle size is a reference and condition/labor can move an exact quote lower or higher.
- Quote language explains why a cleaner SUV may price below a standard SUV package reference.
- Calls to action consistently use **Get an Exact Quote** where condition-based pricing is the appropriate next step.

### Database compatibility

The guest quote feature adds optional guest contact fields to `QuoteThread`, makes `QuoteThread.userId` nullable, and adds a Twilio Message SID field to `QuoteMessage` for deduplication. The quote system includes an idempotent compatibility setup that creates/updates the required columns and indexes on the existing database; it does not wipe existing customers, quotes, bookings, staff roles, warranties, or messages.

### Existing systems preserved

v5.4.0 is based on v5.3.2 and retains the customer dashboard, booking system, staff guide, owner permission management, custom roles, discount controls, two-way SMS, business phone, voicemail, call blocking, call screening, Discord notifications, warranties, gallery/CMS tools, and responsive/mobile navigation.

## Deployment

No new environment variables are required for v5.4.0. Keep the existing pooled Prisma/Vercel and Twilio environment variables from the current production setup.

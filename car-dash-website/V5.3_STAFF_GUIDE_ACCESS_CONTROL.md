# Car Dash Detailing v5.3.0 — Staff Guide, Roles & Account Control

## New Staff Guide

A new internal **Staff Guide** panel is available to the Owner and any staff account with the `Staff Guide` permission. Standard Admin accounts receive this permission by default.

The guide includes:

- Car Dash rules and non-negotiable standards
- Professionalism and customer-service expectations
- Support ticket workflow, first replies, escalation language, and closing replies
- Voice-call opening and closing scripts
- Exact-quote chat guidance
- Booking-chat guidance
- SMS inbox guidance
- Owner-escalation rules for damage claims, refunds, chargebacks, warranty disputes, legal/privacy issues, and safety concerns
- Searchable sections and copy-to-clipboard scripts
- Quick links to the staff panels the logged-in account is actually allowed to use

The guide is permission-checked on the server before its internal content is returned.

## Staff & Accounts

The previous Admin Accounts area is now **Owner → Staff & Accounts**.

The Owner can:

- Search every account by name, email, phone, role, or custom role
- View account creation/update dates
- View booking, vehicle, quote-chat, and warranty counts
- View saved vehicles and recent bookings
- Open the customer's latest booking conversation through Car Dash messaging
- Email the account
- Edit name and email
- Reset a non-owner account password
- Add the standard **Admin** role or remove it
- Assign or unassign custom roles
- Grant or deny individual dashboard permissions
- Remove all staff access with one action
- Permanently delete non-owner accounts

The protected Owner account cannot be demoted, deleted, or have its owner email replaced from this panel.

## Custom roles

The Owner can create reusable roles from scratch or start with built-in templates:

- Detailer
- Support Agent
- Quote Specialist
- Manager
- Content Manager

Templates only prefill the role name, description, and permissions; the Owner can customize everything before saving. Each role can contain any combination of dashboard permissions. Roles can be edited or deleted at any time and assigned to multiple accounts.

## Dashboard permissions

The permission catalog includes:

- Staff Guide
- Bookings & Booking Chat
- SMS Inbox
- Quote Chats
- Support
- Business Phone
- Ceramic Warranties
- Analytics
- Services
- Photos & Media
- Website Editor
- Pricing & Discounts

A standard Admin starts with Staff Guide, Bookings, SMS Inbox, Quote Chats, and Support. The Owner can override any of those permissions per account. A custom-role user does not need the standard Admin role to use staff tools.

Per-account overrides work as:

- **Inherit** — use the standard Admin role and assigned custom roles
- **Allow** — explicitly grant the permission
- **Deny** — explicitly remove it, even if a role normally grants it

## Security / enforcement

Permissions are checked in the API routes that perform privileged work. Removing a permission takes effect on subsequent requests even if that staff member is already signed in. Client route guards also prevent staff from opening dashboard pages they were not assigned.

The permission boundaries follow least privilege: Analytics access does not expose raw booking or quote inbox data, Website Editor access does not silently grant pricing access, and pricing keys are ignored unless the account also has Pricing & Discounts permission.

Destructive operations such as deleting bookings, deleting quote conversations, deleting support conversations, repairing Twilio webhooks, managing roles, and deleting accounts remain Owner-only where appropriate.

## Database behavior

v5.3.0 adds three small access-control tables:

- `CustomRole`
- `CustomRoleAssignment`
- `UserPermissionOverride`

They are included in the Prisma schema and are also created safely with `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` the first time the new access-control system is used. This avoids requiring a destructive database reset or manual `db push` for the production data already in the site.

## Existing accounts

Existing customers remain normal customer accounts.

Existing Admin accounts automatically receive the default Admin permissions listed above. The Owner can immediately change those permissions from **Staff & Accounts**.

## Staff account page

Staff members keep access to the normal **Account** page so they can update their own profile/password and log out. Their Account page shows their base role, assigned custom roles, and a read-only summary of which staff permissions they currently have. It does not expose the all-customer booking feed.

## Route behavior

- `/admin/*` routes require staff access and, where applicable, the matching permission.
- Shared `/owner/*` operational tools such as Business Phone, Warranties, Analytics, Services, Gallery, Website Editor, and Pricing can be opened by staff only when the matching permission is assigned.
- Owner-only areas such as Owner Dashboard, Staff & Accounts, settings, and setup remain owner-only.

## Deployment

- No new environment variables are required for v5.3.0.
- Keep the existing pooled database configuration from v5.2.1+.
- No destructive database reset is required.
- After deployment, sign in as Owner and open **Staff & Accounts** once. The access-control tables are created safely if they do not already exist.
- Existing Admin accounts keep working and receive the default Admin permission set automatically.

## v5.3 final review notes

The final review also separates Ceramic Warranty vehicle lookup from Quote Chat access, so a warranty-only staff role can select customer vehicles without being granted access to quote conversations. Staff accounts also retain the Account page for profile/password management and logout.
